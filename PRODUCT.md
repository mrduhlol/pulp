# Pulp
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
Web extension for Chrome and Firefox.
## Purpose
Adjust music playback with an Android equalizer inspired interface.
## Brand commitments
True black background, rounded and contained edges, modern typography and custom audio controls. The user rejected mango-colored panels, serif headings and stock slider styling. Accent color is restrained.
## Stack
TypeScript compiled with esbuild; plain HTML and CSS.
## Constraints
Protected media and cross-origin players are unsupported. No analytics or remote services.
